Privacy Policy

Your trust is important to us. This policy explains how Hawthorne Digital collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you visit our website or use our services.

1. Introduction

Hawthorne Digital ("we", "us" or "our") is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting the personal information you provide to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, contact us, enquire about our services or become a customer.

We aim to handle all personal information fairly, lawfully and transparently, in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation, including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation, Hawthorne Digital acts as the data controller where it determines why and how personal information is processed.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect and process the following categories of personal information:

Contact Information

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number.
  • Your business or organisation name.
  • Other contact details you choose to provide.

Enquiry and Project Information

  • Details about your website or digital project.
  • Your service requirements and preferences.
  • Information included in messages and enquiries.
  • Project correspondence and agreed requirements.
  • Relevant files or documents you voluntarily provide.

Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the website features and services in use.

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device and operating system information.
  • Website pages visited.
  • General website usage information.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information through our general website enquiry forms.

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect information through several different methods, including:

  • When you complete a contact or enquiry form on our website.
  • When you email or otherwise communicate with our team.
  • When you request a quotation or information about our services.
  • When you enter into an agreement with us or become a customer.
  • When you provide information during an ongoing project or support request.
  • Through technical tools and website technologies where applicable.

We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes and where we have an appropriate lawful basis to do so.

This may include:

  • Responding to enquiries and messages.
  • Preparing quotations and proposals.
  • Providing website design and digital services.
  • Managing projects and customer relationships.
  • Providing technical assistance and support.
  • Communicating important service updates.
  • Managing payments, invoices and administration.
  • Improving our website and services.
  • Maintaining business and security records.
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations.

Our Lawful Bases for Processing

Depending on the circumstances, we may process your information using one or more of the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: Where processing is necessary to enter into or fulfil an agreement with you.
  • Legitimate interests: Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided those interests do not override your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation: Where we need to process information to comply with applicable laws.
  • Consent: Where you have given permission for a particular processing activity.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

5. Sharing Your Information

We respect the confidentiality of your information and do not sell, rent or trade personal information to third parties.

However, in order to operate our business and provide our services, we may need to share relevant information with trusted third-party providers.

These may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email and communication service providers.
  • Website form and enquiry processing providers.
  • Payment and accounting service providers.
  • Website security and technical service providers.
  • Professional advisers where appropriate.

Third-party providers should only receive information that is reasonably necessary for the services they provide.

We may also disclose information where required by law, where necessary to protect our legal rights or where required by an appropriate regulatory authority.

International Transfers

Some service providers may process or store information outside the United Kingdom.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that the transfer complies with applicable data protection requirements.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, security and performance.

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on your device when you visit a website.

Types of Cookies

  • Essential cookies: These may be necessary for basic website functionality and security.
  • Performance cookies: These may help us understand how visitors use our website.
  • Analytics cookies: These may help us improve website performance and user experience.

Where cookies or similar technologies require consent under applicable law, we will seek your permission before using them.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of a website function.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against accidental loss, unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Our security measures may include:

  • Appropriate access restrictions.
  • Secure account and system management.
  • Security monitoring where applicable.
  • Appropriate software and system updates.
  • Use of trusted service providers.
  • Reasonable measures to protect stored information.

Access to personal information should be limited to individuals who need it for legitimate business purposes.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

The period for which information is retained may depend on:

  • The nature of the information.
  • The reason it was collected.
  • Whether we have an ongoing business relationship.
  • Legal, accounting or regulatory requirements.
  • Whether information is required to resolve a dispute.

When personal information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete it or otherwise dispose of it.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable UK data protection legislation, you may have several rights in relation to your personal information.

These may include:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing: Request that we limit how your information is used in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: Request certain information in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: Object to certain types of processing, including processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Rights relating to automated decisions: Have certain rights where decisions are made solely through automated processing with legal or similarly significant effects.

These rights are subject to certain conditions and exemptions under applicable legislation.

If you wish to exercise any of your rights, please contact us using the details below.

You also have the right to raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
