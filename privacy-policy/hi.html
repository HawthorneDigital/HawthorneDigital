Your Privacy
Matters to Us.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices available to you.

Privacy Policy • Last updated: 13 November 2026

A straightforward approach to your privacy

When you contact us, use our website or enquire about our services, you may share information with us. We only use personal information where we have a lawful reason to do so, and we take reasonable steps to keep it secure.

01 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Hawthorne Digital, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name and contact details, including email address and telephone number.
  • Your business details and website address.
  • Information you provide through enquiry forms, emails or other communications.
  • Technical information about your visit to our website, such as your IP address, browser and device information, where collected.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

02 How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotations.
  • Deliver and manage services you have requested.
  • Communicate with you about projects, support and account-related matters.
  • Maintain, protect and improve our website and services.
  • Meet legal, regulatory and business record-keeping obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We will only send marketing communications where we have an appropriate lawful basis or your consent where required. You can opt out at any time.

03 Sharing and Storing Your Information

We may use trusted third-party providers to help operate our website and deliver services. These may include website hosting, email, form processing, analytics and other technical providers.

Where providers process personal information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place. Information may be processed outside the UK; where this happens, applicable data protection transfer requirements will be followed.

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including any legal or accounting requirements. We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to help protect it.

04 Cookies and Website Technology

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functions, understand website performance or improve your experience.

Where consent is required, non-essential cookies will only be used after you have given permission. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

05 Your Privacy Rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Object to or ask us to restrict certain types of processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on your consent.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format.

These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions. You also have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

06 Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, practices or legal obligations. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated revision date.

If you have a question about this policy, want to exercise your privacy rights or have a concern about how your information is handled, please contact us using the details below.
