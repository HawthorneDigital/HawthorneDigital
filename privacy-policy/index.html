SECTION 01

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Hawthorne Digital collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us or use our services.

Hawthorne Digital is committed to handling personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation, Hawthorne Digital acts as the data controller for the personal information described in this policy, where applicable.

Our approach: We aim to collect only the information we need, use it responsibly and explain clearly how it is handled.

SECTION 02

Information We Collect

The personal information we collect depends on how you interact with Hawthorne Digital. This may include:

Information you provide to us

  • Your name and business name.
  • Your email address and telephone number.
  • Information submitted through our contact or enquiry forms.
  • Project details, requirements and communications.
  • Billing and payment-related information where necessary.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically. This may include your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and general website usage information.

We may also receive information from service providers where this is necessary to deliver our services or operate our website.

SECTION 03

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only where we have a valid reason to do so. Depending on the circumstances, this may include:

  • Responding to enquiries and messages.
  • Preparing quotations and proposals.
  • Providing and managing our services.
  • Communicating with clients about ongoing projects.
  • Managing payments, invoices and business records.
  • Maintaining, improving and securing our website.
  • Meeting legal, regulatory and administrative obligations.
  • Sending relevant business communications where permitted.

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes that are incompatible with the reason it was originally collected. If we need to use it for a new purpose, we will provide further information where required.

SECTION 04

Lawful Bases for Processing

Under UK GDPR, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal information. Depending on the situation, we may rely on the following lawful bases:

Contract

Processing may be necessary to provide a service you have requested, prepare for a contract or fulfil our contractual responsibilities.

Legitimate interests

We may process information where it is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided those interests do not override your rights and freedoms.

Legal obligation

We may need to process or retain information to comply with applicable laws and legal requirements.

Consent

Where consent is required, we will ask for it before carrying out the relevant processing. You can withdraw your consent at any time.

SECTION 05

Sharing and Retaining Information

We do not sell personal information. However, we may share information with trusted third-party providers where necessary to operate our business and deliver our services.

These providers may include:

  • Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Email and communication service providers.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Website development and technical service providers.
  • Professional advisers, where necessary.

We expect appropriate safeguards to be in place when personal information is shared with service providers. We may also disclose information where required by law or a valid legal request.

International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that it receives a level of protection required by applicable data protection legislation.

How long we keep information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including any legal, accounting or reporting requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and our reason for holding it. When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or securely dispose of it.

SECTION 06

Cookies and Marketing

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to help it function, understand website usage and improve the visitor experience.

Some cookies may be essential for the operation of the website. Where required, we will request your consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Please be aware that disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of a website function.

Marketing communications

We may send relevant updates or marketing communications where we are legally permitted to do so. Where consent is required, we will obtain it first.

You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided or by contacting us. Opting out of marketing will not affect essential service communications.

SECTION 07

Your Rights and Data Security

Under applicable data protection legislation, you may have several rights regarding your personal information.

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing: Ask us to limit how we use your information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: Request certain information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Right to object: Object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing.
  • Rights relating to automated decisions: You may have rights where decisions are made solely by automated means and have legal or similarly significant effects.

These rights are subject to certain conditions and exceptions. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request. We aim to respond within the time limits required by law.

Keeping information secure

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, accidental loss, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security.

If you have concerns about how your personal information is being handled, please contact us so we can investigate.

SECTION 08

Other Information and Contact

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals who are able to enter into relevant service arrangements. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children where doing so would be inappropriate.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

External websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or third-party services. These websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, business practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be published on this page, together with a revised update date.
