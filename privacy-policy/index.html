This Privacy Policy applies to visitors to our website, individuals who contact us, prospective customers, existing customers and anyone whose personal information we process while providing our services.

Please read this policy carefully so that you understand how your information is handled.

01Who We Are

Hawthorne Digital is a UK-based digital services business providing website design, website development, hosting, website support, domains, email services and graphic design.

For the purposes of applicable UK data protection legislation, Hawthorne Digital is responsible for determining how and why personal information is processed where we act as the data controller.

Throughout this policy, references to "we", "us" and "our" mean Hawthorne Digital.

02Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect and process the following types of personal information:

  • Contact information: Your name, email address, telephone number and business address.
  • Business information: Your business name, website details and information relevant to the services you request.
  • Communication information: Messages, enquiries, feedback and correspondence you send to us.
  • Customer information: Details relating to quotations, orders, projects, invoices and services.
  • Technical information: Information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and website usage data, where collected.

We only seek to collect information that is relevant to the purpose for which it is required.

03How We Collect Your Information

We may collect personal information in several ways, including:

  • When you complete a contact, enquiry or quotation form on our website.
  • When you email, telephone or otherwise contact our team.
  • When you purchase or enquire about one of our services.
  • During the delivery and management of a project or customer relationship.
  • Through cookies and similar technologies, where enabled on our website.
  • From publicly available business information where appropriate and lawful.

We may also receive information from third-party providers involved in delivering services to you, where this is necessary and lawful.

04How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries and requests for information.
  • Preparing quotations and providing our services.
  • Communicating with customers about projects and services.
  • Managing customer accounts, payments and invoices.
  • Providing technical support and resolving problems.
  • Maintaining and improving our website and services.
  • Protecting our systems, business and customers from misuse, fraud and security threats.
  • Meeting our legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

We will not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without a valid lawful basis. Where required, we will ask for your permission before sending direct marketing communications.

05Our Lawful Basis for Processing

Under UK data protection legislation, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal information. Depending on the circumstances, we may rely on:

  • Contract: Where processing is necessary to provide a service or take steps at your request before entering into a contract.
  • Legitimate interests: Where processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests and these do not override your rights and freedoms.
  • Legal obligation: Where we need to process information to comply with applicable laws.
  • Consent: Where you have given permission for a specific purpose, such as certain marketing or non-essential cookies.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before its withdrawal.

06Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support its functionality, understand how visitors use the website and improve the experience we provide.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Some cookies may be necessary for the website to operate, while others may be used for analytics or additional functionality.

Where required, we will request your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of our website function.

07Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share relevant information with trusted third parties where necessary to operate our business and provide our services.

These may include:

  • Website hosting, domain and email service providers.
  • Payment processors and financial service providers.
  • Software, website and infrastructure providers.
  • Professional advisers, where appropriate.
  • Authorities or regulatory bodies where we are legally required to do so.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we take appropriate steps to ensure that suitable safeguards and contractual arrangements are in place.

If information is transferred outside the UK, we will ensure that an appropriate legal transfer mechanism and safeguards are used where required.

08How We Store and Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

These measures may include access controls, appropriate security practices, secure service providers and safeguards designed to protect the information we hold.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including where retention is required to meet legal, accounting or reporting obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to securely delete or dispose of it.

09Your Data Protection Rights

Under applicable UK data protection legislation, you may have a number of rights regarding your personal information. These include:

  • Right of access: To request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: To ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure: To request the deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restrict processing: To ask us to limit how we use your information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: To request certain information in a commonly used electronic format.
  • Right to object: To object to certain types of processing, including direct marketing.
  • Rights relating to automated decisions: To have certain decisions involving automated processing reviewed where applicable.

These rights are subject to certain conditions and exemptions. We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

You can exercise your rights by contacting us using the details in the final section of this policy.
