Who We Are

Technology should open doors, not create barriers.

Hawthorne Digital was built around a straightforward idea: professional digital services should be accessible to businesses of all sizes, not just those with large budgets.

We work with businesses to create websites and digital solutions that reflect who they are, what they do and where they want to go. Whether you're starting from scratch, refreshing an existing website or looking for ongoing support, we aim to make the process clear, collaborative and straightforward.

Based in Staffordshire and working with businesses throughout the UK, we combine a personal approach with the flexibility to deliver solutions suited to each individual client.

Our clients and partners are at the heart of everything we do. We believe the best results come from working together.
