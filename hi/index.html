Who We Are

Technology should open doors, not create barriers.

At Hawthorne Digital, we believe every business deserves access to professional digital services without the premium price tag.

The online world is an important part of how businesses connect with people. A well-designed website can communicate your identity, build confidence and help customers understand what makes your business different.

We work with businesses to create digital experiences that are considered, practical and tailored to their needs. We don't believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Every project starts with understanding the people and business behind it.

Our work extends beyond website design. Through hosting, support, domains, email and other digital services, we aim to make managing your online presence simpler.

Based in Staffordshire and working with businesses across the United Kingdom, we combine an approachable service with a professional standard of work.
