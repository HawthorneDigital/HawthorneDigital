WHO WE ARE

Making digital work for everyone.

Hawthorne Digital was built around a simple idea: professional digital services should be available to businesses of every size, not just those with large budgets.

We provide a range of digital services designed to help businesses establish, improve and manage their online presence. From website design and web applications to ongoing support and graphic design, our focus is on finding solutions that work for the people using them.

We understand that every business has different priorities. That's why we take the time to understand your requirements, communicate clearly and involve you throughout the process.

Based in Staffordshire and working with businesses across the UK, we're focused on building positive working relationships and delivering practical results.
