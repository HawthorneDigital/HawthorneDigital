Our Story

Built on a simple idea.

Professional digital services should be accessible, understandable and built around the people using them.

Hawthorne Digital was established with a clear ambition: to make professional digital services more accessible to businesses without compromising on presentation, quality or customer experience.

We recognised that finding the right digital support can be challenging. Businesses often need more than just a website. They need a reliable point of contact, straightforward advice and solutions that can develop alongside their organisation.

That is why Hawthorne Digital brings different digital services together under one roof. From initial ideas and website development through to ongoing support, our aim is to make the process easier to understand and manage.

We continue to develop our services, explore new technologies and improve the way we work, always keeping the needs of our clients and partners at the centre of our decisions.

We are here to make digital work better for the businesses behind it.
