Welcome to Tivvabloom

A little place with a lot of heart.

Childhood is a time for curiosity, imagination and discovery. Our nursery concept is built around creating a warm and welcoming environment where every child can explore at their own pace.

We believe that meaningful relationships, playful experiences and thoughtful learning opportunities help children develop the confidence to take their next steps.

From those very first milestones to preparing for school, we celebrate the little things that make every child's journey special.

What ages does the nursery cater for?

This nursery concept is designed for children aged three months to five years, with environments tailored to each developmental stage.

Can parents arrange a nursery visit?

The demonstration enquiry form illustrates how families could request a visit, meet the team and explore the nursery environment.

How do settling-in sessions work?

Settling-in arrangements can be adapted to each child's needs, helping them develop familiarity and confidence with their new surroundings.

How are dietary requirements managed?

In an operational nursery, dietary needs and allergies should be discussed with families and managed through appropriate food safety procedures.

What about funded childcare?

Eligibility depends on current government rules and family circumstances. This fictional nursery does not offer actual funded childcare places.

How can parents stay informed?

Daily updates, conversations and scheduled progress discussions are examples of how a nursery can maintain communication with families.

Is Tivvabloom a real nursery?

No. Tivvabloom is a fictional nursery brand created by Hawthorne Digital to demonstrate website design and functionality.
