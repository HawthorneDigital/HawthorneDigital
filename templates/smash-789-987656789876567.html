Need to know?

Good questions.

Everything you need to know about this demonstration ordering experience.

Can I customise my burger?

Yes. Select a burger and choose available extras before adding it to your basket. Your total updates automatically.

Can I actually place an order?

No. SMASH 789 is a fictional portfolio website. The ordering system demonstrates functionality without submitting real orders.

Do you offer collection and delivery?

Both options are included in the demonstration. Delivery adds an illustrative £2.49 charge.

Where can I find allergen information?

This is an illustrative menu, not verified food or allergen information. A live restaurant website would need accurate ingredient and allergen details supplied by the restaurant.

Will I be charged at checkout?

No. The checkout is a demonstration only. No payment information is requested or processed.
