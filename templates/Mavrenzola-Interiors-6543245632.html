Helpful Information

Curious about the details?

Find answers to common questions about interior design, renovations and working with a design studio.

Can you design just one room?

Our design approach can be adapted to a single room, several rooms or an entire property. The level of support depends on your requirements.

Do I need to know my preferred design style?

No. Exploring different styles, materials and colour palettes is an important part of the initial design consultation.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Existing furniture, artwork and meaningful possessions can be incorporated into a new design concept wherever appropriate.

What happens during the initial consultation?

The initial consultation explores your property, lifestyle, practical requirements, preferred design direction, budget and anticipated timescale.

How long does an interior design project take?

Timescales depend on the size and complexity of the project, availability of materials, design revisions and any associated renovation work.

Can you design commercial spaces?

Our illustrative service offering includes commercial interiors for offices, hospitality environments and retail spaces.

Is the online budget estimate a final quotation?

No. The estimator demonstrates how different project requirements can influence design fees. An actual quotation would require a detailed assessment of the proposed work.
