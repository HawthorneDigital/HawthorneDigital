Should I learn manual or automatic?

Manual lessons involve learning clutch control and gear changes. Automatic lessons remove these tasks. The right choice depends on your preferences and driving goals.

Can I choose my instructor?

Yes. The demonstration booking system displays fictional instructors based on your selected lesson type.

How long are the lessons?

You can select a one-hour, 90-minute or two-hour lesson. Your price updates automatically based on your selection.

Is the availability real?

No. The calendar and available times are simulated to demonstrate how a live driving lesson booking system could work.

Do I need to pay online?

No payment is requested or processed. DRIVE 789 is a fictional portfolio website and does not provide actual driving lessons.
