Frequently Asked Questions

A little more
about the process.

Helpful information for anyone considering an interior design or home renovation project.

Can you design a single room?

Our design approach can be adapted to individual rooms, multiple spaces or complete property transformations.

Do I need to have a design style in mind?

Not at all. The initial consultation helps explore your preferences and establish a design direction that reflects your lifestyle.

Can existing furniture be incorporated?

Existing furniture, artwork and personal possessions can form an important part of a new interior design concept.

How much does interior design cost?

Costs depend on the scope of the project, the level of design support required and the proposed materials and furnishings.

How long does the design process take?

Timescales vary depending on project size, design requirements, material availability and the extent of any renovation work.
