Explore the buffet

Something for every appetite.

Dishes rotate and are subject to availability. Photography is illustrative.

Starters & sides

Satay chicken skewers, salt & pepper wings, Peking ribs, crispy wontons, vegetable spring rolls and onion rings (V).

Signature mains

Aromatic duck with pancakes, crispy chilli beef, butterfly king prawns, Cantonese fillet steak, sweet & sour chicken and white fish with soy sauce.

Rice, noodles & extras

Egg fried rice, vegetarian Singapore rice, Amoy-style vermicelli, international dishes and fresh salad.

Chocolate fountain

Unlimited soft ice cream

Enjoy the soft ice cream machine during evening sessions.

From the bar

Draft beer including Cobra, wine, spirits, soft drinks and coffee.
