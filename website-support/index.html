Introducing Bracket

A simpler way to manage website support.

Bracket is Hawthorne Digital's support system, designed to help organise website-related requests and make it easier to follow their progress.

Rather than relying on scattered messages, Bracket provides a structured approach to managing requests, keeping information organised and showing how work is progressing.

Please note: Bracket is currently temporarily unavailable while upgrades are being completed. During this period, support enquiries can be sent directly to our team.

Support made more straightforward.

Bracket is designed around three simple request statuses, making it easier to understand the progress of support work.

◷
Awaiting Your request has been received and is waiting to be reviewed or actioned.
↻
In Progress Your request is being worked on by the relevant team.
✓
Resolved The requested work has been completed or the reported issue addressed.
