Frequently asked questions

A few things you might want to know.

If you have a question that isn't covered here, we're always happy to discuss your project.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different, so the cost depends on the size of the website, its functionality and the level of customisation required. We provide quotes based on your specific requirements, so you know what you're paying for before work begins.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timescales depend on the scope and complexity of the project, as well as how quickly content, feedback and approvals are provided. We'll discuss an estimated timeframe with you before the project gets underway.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Mobile-friendly design is a standard part of our website design approach. We consider different screen sizes so your website can adapt to mobiles, tablets and desktops.

Can I update my own website?

Depending on the platform and solution chosen, we can provide a content management system that allows you to make suitable updates yourself. We'll discuss the best approach for how you want to manage your website.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. We can discuss refreshing an existing website, improving its layout and appearance, or rebuilding it around your current business requirements.

Is hosting included in the website design price?

Hosting is a separate service and can be discussed as part of your project. If you already have a hosting provider, we can consider your existing arrangements too.

Do you provide SEO?

We consider good technical and structural foundations when building websites. Full SEO services, campaigns and ongoing optimisation are separate requirements and can be quoted for independently.

Do you work with businesses outside Staffordshire?

Absolutely. Hawthorne Digital is based in Staffordshire and works with businesses throughout the UK. We can discuss projects remotely, wherever you're based.
